Projector

Canvas 2D rendering and physics simulation demos.

  • Cube container Thousands of spheres inside a box you can spin and throw around.
  • Gravitation N-body attraction with collisions: a disk of bodies orbiting a heavy core.
  • Cloth Distance-constrained particles in Perlin noise wind, flat shaded.
  • Lighting Colored point and spot lights, smooth shading, highlights, planar shadows and fog on a 2D canvas.
  • Flow field 4000 particles carried by an evolving curl-noise field built from 3D Perlin noise.
